Case 02

One wish

“If I Had One Wish”, an integrated fundraising campaign

MSD United Way Vietnam, December 2023 to February 2024

  • Fundraising
  • Campaign
  • Event

Context

The annual campaign “15 Years Together: A New Page of Life” raised funds for children without birth certificates and children in difficult circumstances in Ho Chi Minh City. It ran on Facebook, TikTok, with KOLs and in the press, and closed with the event “If I Had One Wish” on 20 January 2024.

My role

Communications lead and fundraising coordinator.

What I did

  • Wrote the event script and designed the key visual and campaign collaterals.
  • Coordinated fundraising from individuals and companies: merchandise sales, a donation booth, crowdfunding and in-kind gifts.

Results

  • 501,753,070 VND raised: 258M VND in cash and 243.7M VND in kind.
  • 124K+ reach, 3.3K+ engagements and 48K+ video views.
  • 20+ press outlets and KOLs shared the campaign, with 7 campaign ambassadors.
  • 40+ individual donors and 20+ partner organisations.
  • 100 children received Tet gifts at the event and 3 received their birth certificates.
